Tracking confidence
—Waiting for selections

Estimated geometric tracking quality, from feature agreement, match count, and alignment error. This is not the probability that an object is a lug nut.

How the score is calculated

100 × min(1, matches ÷ 60) × inlier ratio × exp(−alignment error ÷ 5). It measures the shared wheel motion. Object names come from your initial selections.
